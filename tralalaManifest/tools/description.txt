Manifest - TRALALA, lyrics video where the entire visual is code: no footage, no editor. A Three.js infinity-mirror club corridor in deep purple, electric cyan and neon fuchsia, with calm kinetic typography (Montserrat Bold) timed to the vocal phrases. Fully deterministic: the same song time always renders the same frame, and the lights only breathe softly with the 125 BPM kick (no flashing, no camera shake).

TECHNICAL DETAILS
• Rendering: Three.js (WebGL) + GLSL shaders + Canvas 2D typography, bloom post-processing
• Audio analysis in the browser: tempo/beat grid (125 BPM), kick/bass envelopes drive the light pulse and camera speed
• Lyric timing: word-level timestamps from the audio (faster-whisper), grouped into vocal phrase blocks, official lyrics text applied on top
• Capture: Playwright renders every frame losslessly (PNG), FFmpeg encodes 1920x1080 / 30 fps H.264 + AAC
